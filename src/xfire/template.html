About the data & harmonization

XFiRE combines NASA FIRMS active-fire detections from MODIS and VIIRS. Because the sensors differ in resolution and sensitivity, VIIRS observations are adjusted to a MODIS-equivalent scale using their overlapping observation period. This creates a more consistent long-term activity index for comparing months and years.

Hotspots indicate satellite-detected thermal anomalies, not confirmed fire perimeters. Results depend on sensor coverage, cloud conditions, and the selected area. Use the calendar as a monitoring and comparison aid, not as a substitute for official incident reports.
